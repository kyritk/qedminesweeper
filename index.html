QED Minesweeper

A quick brain workout. An endless board. No guessing.

Get it on Google PlayPlay in the browser (iPhone, PC)

  • A board without edges, opened by logic alone. Every chunk is solver-verified before it appears, so no position ever needs a guess
  • Step on one mine and the world ends. The cells you opened are your score
  • On Android, compete for this week’s rank on the Google Play Games leaderboard. Resets every Monday
  • Nine themes (glass, forest, iron, cute, pixel, sky, space, washi and ink, and more), unlocked with points earned by playing. No in-app purchases
  • The browser version is for iPhone and PC: no ads, no leaderboard. Your save lives in that browser; on Safari, “Add to Home Screen” keeps it from being cleared
